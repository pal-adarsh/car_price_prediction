AdaBoost Underperformance (R² = 0.4589)

AdaBoost's default loss sequentially inflates weights for high-error outliers. Because selling prices feature luxury supercars (up to ₹3.95 Cr) and mileage outliers (>3.8M km), boosting concentrates capacity on fitting extreme outliers rather than standard vehicles.

SVR Dual Standardization (R² = 0.6898)

SVR utilizes RBF distance metrics. Without scaling target vector y, kernel optimization destabilizes and yields negative R². Standardizing both X and y with inverse transformation achieves R² = 0.6898.
